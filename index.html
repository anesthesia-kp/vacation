Rules & Reminders

  • The auction has 4 phases. Phase 1 is for certain high-demand weeks only; all other weeks open in Phase 2.
  • Phase 4 is the Rolling Vacation Auction that has rounds periodically throughout 2027. Each round functions as its own phase.
  • Running cap on total weeks you can win across the auction — only weeks you win count
  • You hold bid numbers 1 to 10, plus NP — 1 is the strongest bid and NP the weakest
  • Each number can be used once; numbers on weeks you won earlier can't be used again.
  • Bids that do not win are returned to you when the next phase begins.
  • A combined bid like 1/2/3 uses bids 1, 2, and 3 at once
  • NP bids may only be used during the phases for which admin has turned them on
  • Bid priorities can only stay the same or get stronger — bid a 5 on a week, and any re-bid there must be 5 or better.
  • To swap bids between two of your weeks, use ✎ Edit, then ⇄ Swap bids.
  • A week becomes a “Competition week” once the people bidding on it at the same time have a greater combined FTE than it has available. It remains one for the rest of the phase.
  • On a competition week, a new bid must match or beat the weakest winning or under-review bid, and you can only weaken your bid (re-bids included) if it would still win and is no lower than the lowest other winning bid. If everyone leaves, a new bid must match or beat the last bid that was there.
  • Bidder names are hidden: only your own bids show initials.

  • E-mail alerts tell you when someone else's bid changes your projection on a week you bid on, and when a bid you withdrew would now win. Turn either off under E-mail alerts.
  • Week out of reach? Use “Notify if a week opens up” under E-mail alerts with the bid you'd place. If that bid could later win or be under review, you'll be e-mailed and the countdown restarts so you have time to bid. Lasts until the phase ends.
  • Projections are not final — they can change as others bid or staffing needs change.
