📋 Rules & Reminders — click to expand and review
▼
  • Vacation selections are now called "bids" — the process works like an auction, where bids compete for limited slots based on priority
  • Enter your bids early, it benefits everyone
  • Sniping is not possible, do not try it
  • Only 3 bids (including NP bids) may be used per phase — only bid for the number of weeks your accrued vacation and ATO will allow for
  • Bid priorities can only stay the same or increase, never decrease — once you bid 5 on a week, you can re-bid only 5 or better
  • On the highest demand weeks (Ski Week, Spring Break, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's), only bids of 1, 2, 3, 4, 1,2, or 1,2,3 may be used
  • NP bids may only be used during the phases for which admin has turned them on
  • To maximize your own benefit, consider starting with lower bids and increasing as necessary to prevent "overpaying" for a week
⏱ Timer Rules
  • Understanding Outcomes
Winning (W)
Your bid is projected to be approved for this week
Draw (D)
You are tied with one or more people and combined FTE exceeds availability; a spin-off or admin approval will resolve the tie
Under Review (R)
Your bid is slightly over the cap; admin may still approve under certain circumstances
Losing (L)
Your bid is projected not to be approved for this week
  • You will be notified via e-mail when someone places a bid that changes your outcome for a week
  • If you are in a Draw or Under Review for a week and do not win after it is resolved, you will get the bid back for the next phase
  • These are projections, not final results — outcomes can shift as others place bids or as unexpected staffing needs change
